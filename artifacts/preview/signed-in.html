Demo mode

This is a copy of the site: a demo guild, Alphabet Mafia on PC / NA Megaserver, with guildmates who keep items here so there is somebody to ask and somebody to answer. Everything works - reserving, cancelling, sending, answering, changing accounts - but only inside the copy. While this page is open those guildmates ask, hand over and re-save their own items, so you can watch the site being used; that stops when the demo is closed. No count changes for anybody else, no email is ever sent, uploading is switched off and the password cannot be reset. Sign up and upload your own file to take part for real.

One step before the list

There is no ESO account behind this sign-in yet, so there are no guilds to compare against and nothing the site could safely show you. Upload the file the ItemShare addon writes and every account in it is attached to you; after that, what your guildmates share appears here.
